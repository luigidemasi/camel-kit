This article highlights the releases for the Red Hat build of Apache Camel family of products.

Red Hat aims to have one minor release of Red Hat build of Apache Camel 4.x around every six months, typically, but not necessarily, aligning to upstream long term support (LTS) versions of Apache Camel. That six months cadence is an estimate, not a strict rule.

Micro level releases of Red Hat build of Apache Camel 4.x.y do not follow a strict release cadence and are planned based on critical bug fixes and important security vulnerabilities (CVEs).

**Note:** 1. Check the life cycle dates of Red Hat build of Apache Camel [here](https://access.redhat.com/support/policy/updates/jboss_notes/#p_rhbocamel) . 2. You can further follow KCS article ["How to setup alert and notifications about JBoss software updates?"](https://access.redhat.com/solutions/36012) to be notified automatically about any new releases of Red Hat build of Apache Camel.
